Jumpy: Bounce & Climb — Support

Developer: Firyal Hawwary

Jumpy: Bounce & Climb is a free, fully offline bouncing game for kids and families. Steer a cute character as it bounces higher and higher, land on platforms, grab stars, and race the rising water.

How to play

  • Hold the left or right side of the screen to steer. Your character bounces by itself.
  • Prefer tilting? Open Settings (the gear on the title screen) and turn on Tilt.
  • Blue trampolines give a big boost; rockets, shields and magnets help you climb.
  • Stars you collect unlock new characters, hats and trails in the shop.

Common questions

  • Where is my progress saved? Only on your device. Deleting the app removes it; there is no account or cloud backup.
  • Are there purchases or ads? Not in the current version. If ads are ever added they will be age-appropriate, non-personalised and never shown during play, and any purchase will sit behind the parental gate.
  • Does the game need internet? No. It works completely offline.
  • How do I turn the music or sound off? Use the toggles in Settings on the title screen.
  • Where is the Info & Privacy screen? Behind the grown-ups-only gate on the title screen.
