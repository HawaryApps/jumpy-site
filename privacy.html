Privacy Policy — Jumpy: Bounce & Climb

Last updated: 9 November 2026 · Developer: Firyal Hawwary

Jumpy: Bounce & Climb ("Jumpy", "the app", "we") is a game made for children and families. We built it to respect your privacy completely: the app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, and it works entirely offline on your device.

Information we collect

None. Jumpy has no accounts, no login, and no sign-up. We do not ask for, collect, or receive any personal information such as your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers.

Data stored on your device

Your progress — best scores, collected stars, unlocked characters, hats and trails, and mission progress — is saved only on your device using Apple's on-device storage (UserDefaults). This data never leaves the device, is never sent to us or anyone else, and is removed if you delete the app.

No tracking, no profiling

  • Jumpy makes no network connections — nothing is uploaded or downloaded.
  • Jumpy currently shows no advertisements. If that ever changes, only age-appropriate, non-personalised ads that do not profile children will be used, and this page will be updated first.
  • There are no third-party SDKs, analytics, or tracking technologies.
  • We do not use cookies or any form of behavioural profiling.

Children's privacy

Jumpy is designed to be safe for children. Because the app collects no data and has no network features, ads, or external links available to children, it complies with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR-K). Any informational link out of the game is placed behind a parental gate that requires an adult to solve a simple problem.

Purchases

Jumpy is free and currently contains no real-money purchases. Any purchase added in a future version will sit behind a parental gate. In-game "stars" are earned by playing and have no monetary value.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
